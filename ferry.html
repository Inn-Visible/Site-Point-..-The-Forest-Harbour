how the ferry works peer-to-peer. a public broker introduces the two glasses by code, then steps out — the cargo travels encrypted (DTLS) directly phone↔desktop.
no ground touched. nothing is uploaded to any server; files exist only in the two glasses and the wire between them.
both sides sail. once connected, either glass can drop, paste, snap or fetch; the ferry carries both ways.
image cargo wears thumbnails at both ends; photos under 1.5 MB can be seated into the garden (arc.local image note) instead of downloaded; text under 2 MB seats as a plain note.
the pinterest flow: long-press the picture → copy image → ferry → pull clipboard → sailed. or paste its media url → fetch & sail. seven logins, retired.
if the broker sleeps (offline, blocked, unlucky), the ferry says so plainly — retry, or fall back to the old bridge until the wire clears.
